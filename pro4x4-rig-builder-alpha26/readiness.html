CANONICAL REFERENCE COVERAGE

Per-view persisted coverage binds every canonical master to its exact declared owner references, checksums, pack membership, source/rights attestations, evidence-review decisions and any explicit source gaps. Coverage is evidence only and cannot approve or promote a visual.

CANONICAL SOURCE-GAP DECISIONS

Required geometry-source gaps are governed by a dedicated evidence-bound decision. Generic canonical review fields cannot approve a source gap; the decision remains non-production and is invalidated by evidence drift.

CANONICAL MASTER SET REGISTRY

One persisted vehicle-level truth index binds F34, SIDE and R34 canonical masters to the same reference-pack manifest and exposes candidate, reviewer, approval and production-seal progression across the complete master set. It is inspection-only and cannot promote a visual.

GOVERNANCE ATTENTION QUEUE

Persisted inspection/routing snapshots turn reference-pack, WF3 handoff, reviewer, readiness and source-gap state into one actionable staff queue. The queue cannot approve or promote a visual.

CANONICAL VIEW REGISTRY

Persisted canonical master slots are shown with their owner/reference evidence, camera contract, review contract and exact production blockers. Governance-managed pack/master metadata is write-protected from the generic asset editor and may change only through its owning workflow. Staff readiness assessments are fingerprinted against the current evidence and visibly become stale when that evidence changes. Reference photos remain evidence only and never satisfy production readiness.
